# Flipkart Washing Machine Brand Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes washing machine products listed on Flipkart to understand brand positioning, pricing strategies, technology adoption, product differentiation, and customer perception across major brands. The study follows an end-to-end analytics workflow involving data collection, data preparation, feature engineering, exploratory data analysis, and business insight generation.

---

## Objective

To analyze washing machine products across leading brands and identify how manufacturers differentiate themselves through pricing, product features, technology adoption, capacity offerings, and customer ratings within the online marketplace.

## Technical Workflow

### Data Collection

Product information was collected from Flipkart product listings, including pricing, ratings, reviews, specifications, capacity, load type, machine type, and product attributes.

### Data Preparation

The dataset was standardized and prepared for analysis through:

* Duplicate removal
* Missing value handling
* Attribute extraction
* Data type standardization
* Outlier filtering
* Brand-level filtering for reliable comparisons

### Feature Engineering

Several analytical features were created to support business analysis:

* Capacity (kg)
* Machine Type (Automatic / Semi-Automatic)
* Load Type (Front Load / Top Load)
* Energy Star Rating
* Discount Percentage
* Total Feature Count
* AI Feature Indicator
* WiFi Connectivity Indicator
* Steam Wash Indicator
* Heater Indicator
* Inverter Technology Indicator

## Key Insights

### Brand Positioning

* LG achieved the highest average customer rating among analyzed brands.
* IFB demonstrated the highest premium feature adoption.
* Samsung and Haier maintained a balanced position between pricing, features, and customer satisfaction.

### Pricing Analysis

* Products with higher feature counts generally commanded higher selling prices.
* Premium brands combined advanced functionality and brand reputation to justify higher pricing.

### Technology Adoption

* Inverter Technology has become a mainstream feature across the market.
* AI and WiFi connectivity remain premium differentiators and are concentrated within higher-priced products.

### Product Capacity Analysis

* The 8 kg segment emerged as one of the most common capacity categories, with an average selling price of approximately ₹25,000.

### Customer Engagement

* realme TechLife generated significantly higher review volumes despite maintaining a smaller product portfolio, suggesting strong customer adoption within its target price segment.
